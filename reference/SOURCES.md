Google Maps links and embedded maps use full, disambiguated place-name queries,
including locality. They do not represent independently surveyed coordinates.
National park links target the headquarters on the Kamphaeng Phet side rather
than a remote point in the protected area.

## Photographs

Downloaded reference copies are retained locally in reference/photos (excluded
from Git; recreate them with scripts/download_photos.py). Original source links
are recorded in photo-manifest.json. The downloader uses Wikimedia's recommended
thumbnail sizes to reduce transfer volume. WebP derivatives are in
public/images. Derivatives are resized/compressed and displayed with layout
cropping, under the original license. Credits and license links are also visible
in About → Photo credits, and on the attraction detail pages.
